LITTLE SHOP - SETTING UP THE iPAD
For: iPad Air (5th generation, 2022) using Safari
Updated 22 October 2026

HOW IT WORKS
Little Shop is installed once from Safari while the iPad has internet (home Wi-Fi is fine).
After that it lives on the Home Screen as its own app and runs completely offline.
No internet is needed at the markets.

ONE-TIME INSTALL (needs internet, about 2 minutes)
1. Connect the iPad to Wi-Fi.
2. Open Safari and go to the install link you were given.
3. Wait on that page. It says "Preparing the app for offline use", then the shop opens by itself.
4. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, top right of Safari).
5. Tap "Add to Home Screen". If it shows "Open as Web App", leave it switched on.
   Name it "Little Shop" and tap Add. A green shop icon appears on the Home Screen.
6. Close Safari. From now on, always open Little Shop from the green Home Screen icon, not from Safari.
7. Open the green icon once while still on Wi-Fi and let it load.

CHECK IT WORKS OFFLINE (do this before the first market)
1. Swipe down from the top-right corner and turn on Airplane Mode. Make sure Wi-Fi is off too.
2. Swipe up to close the Little Shop app completely, then open it again from the green icon.
3. It should open normally. Try adding an item to the basket and opening Process sale.
4. Turn Airplane Mode off again when you are done.

SETTING UP ITEMS AND MARKETS
Do this inside the Home Screen app (not in Safari). Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate records,
so anything entered in Safari will not appear in the app.
- Editing > Markets: add the markets you sell at.
- Editing > Headings: add headings (e.g. Jams) and subheadings (e.g. Sugar-free).
- Editing > Items: add each item with its name, price, heading and a photo.

AT THE MARKET
1. Open Little Shop from the Home Screen.
2. Tap your market. If you pick the wrong one, tap the market name in the top-right corner to change it.
3. Tap items to add them to the basket. Use - and + to change quantities.
4. Tap Process sale to see the total. Optionally type the cash received (or tap a quick amount) to see the change.
5. Tap Complete sale. The basket clears for the next customer.

KEEP YOUR SETUP SAFE
Your items, headings and markets are saved only on this iPad. They are not backed up anywhere.
- Do NOT delete the Little Shop icon from the Home Screen. Deleting it erases everything entered in it.
- Do NOT use Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data. It can also erase the app's records.
If either happens, reinstall using the steps above and enter the items and markets again.

UPDATES
When a new version of Little Shop is published to the install link, connect the iPad to Wi-Fi,
open Little Shop from the Home Screen, wait a few seconds, close it fully and open it again.
The new version appears and your items and markets stay as they are.
Do not delete the app to update it.

FILES IN THIS FOLDER (for whoever hosts the install link)
- index.html / install-offline.html: the install page that prepares the app
- offline/index.html: the complete app
- offline/sw.js: makes the app work offline
- offline/manifest.webmanifest, offline/icon-192.png, offline/icon-512.png: Home Screen name and icon
- favicon.svg: small browser icon
The whole folder must be uploaded, as it is, to a website with an https:// address.
Opening these files directly from the Files app does NOT work, because iPadOS will not run them there.
